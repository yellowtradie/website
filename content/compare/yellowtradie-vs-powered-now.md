# YellowTradie against Powered Now

Powered Now is a UK trade app that has been around for years. It covers quotes and invoices and jobs and the diary, and it adds certificates and forms and staff tools that many trade firms rely on. That breadth is its strength, and this comparison starts there. If you want forms and team controls in one account today, Powered Now is the more set up choice.

Our verdict is that Powered Now is the better fit for a team that needs forms and routing, and YellowTradie is the better fit for a person on their own who wants the quote to write itself from speech and the records to stay on the phone. The table is for that one person.

Prices checked on 24 October 2026. YellowTradie checked on yellowtradie.com/pricing.html. Powered Now checked on powerednow.com/pricing on the same day.

## When YellowTradie is the better fit

Choose YellowTradie if you want:

- to speak the job on site and have the quote fill itself into Materials and Labour and travel and charges
- a single price of £19.99 a month with no count of users, because there is only you
- your jobs and quotes and invoices kept on your phone, with no second login
- the customer's link to approve a quote without an account or a password
- tax figures worked from your records as an estimate, with the note that it is not advice

## What Powered Now does better

Powered Now has forms and certificates that you can fill on site and send as a PDF. We do not issue certificates, so a plumber or gas engineer who needs that sheet will find it there and not here. The same plan also adds job routing and What3Words and a sketch tool and AI text on Premium. Those are built for a crew that moves between jobs in one day.

It is also built for more than one person. You can add your team, track sickness and holiday, and add staff scheduling and staff tracking. YellowTradie is one login and has no way to pass a job to someone else. If you pay even one other person each week, that difference will be felt each morning.

The Xero link is live there today on Professional and above, and VAT returns are included. Our links are still being built and the site says so. That is not a small gap if your bookkeeper already works in Xero.

## Questions

### What does Powered Now cost for a solo trader today

On powerednow.com/pricing checked on 24 October 2026, Powered Now is £28 a month on Business and £32 on Professional and £40 on Premium, each per user per month and excluding VAT. Annual billing is Business £280 per year and Professional £320 per year and Premium £400 per year. A 14 day trial is offered. YellowTradie checked the same day is £19.99 a month, one plan, 14 days free with a card.

### Can I keep using a laptop in the office

With Powered Now you can. It works on phone and tablet and PC and the same jobs show in each place. YellowTradie keeps jobs on the phone, with a sheet export for the accountant. The customer link is the only page a customer opens outside the phone.

### Does YellowTradie have team features

No. One person on one phone is the whole product. There is no second login and no way to give a job to someone else. If you run a crew of two or more, choose a product that is built for a team.

### How does each app handle a deposit

Both do. In YellowTradie you set the deposit on the quote and the bill for it is raised when the customer approves. In Powered Now you can take deposits and progress payments, with online payments and a mobile card reader on every plan.

### What happens to my records if I cancel

In YellowTradie the records stay on your phone. Cancelling stops the payment and does not delete a quote or an invoice, because we never held a copy. In Powered Now the records are in the cloud, so you need an export if you leave. Check the terms on their site for how long trial data is kept.

### Do I need accounting software with either one

Not to keep the tax figures. Both keep money in and money out. Powered Now moves items into Xero on Professional and above and handles VAT returns. YellowTradie exports a sheet for the accountant and the live links are being built. If a direct Xero feed is a must today, that will be the deciding point.
