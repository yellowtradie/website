# Talking to your app

> **Key facts**
>
> * You must register when taxable turnover goes over £90,000 in a rolling 12 months since 1 April 2024. Read on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.
> * UK VAT has three rates. 20 percent standard and 5 percent reduced and 0 percent zero rated. Read on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.
> * You must issue a VAT invoice within 30 days of supply where you are VAT registered. Read on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.
> * Statutory interest on a late business debt is 8 percent a year above the Bank of England base rate. Read on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.

This post is a day told as a day. One phone and one trader from the morning alarm to the evening bill. Every step is spoken where speaking fits. The quote writes itself and the customer answers on their link and the job and the invoice follow.

## 07.15 Missed call while you are on the scaffold

Your phone is in your pocket. You are holding the drill. The call comes and you cannot answer. The caller leaves a message.

The app does not answer the phone. That line is load bearing. No app should claim to be you on a call.

When a call is missed the caller leaves a message that is written out and sorted and sent to you. The app never pretends to be the trader. That is how the missed calls feature is described on the site. You read the message and you decide if it is a job or a callback or a supplier.

Today it is a job. A landlord with a leak in a flat. You tap the number and you arrange to be there at 10.00. You put busy in the diary for that address so the day shows the block.

## 10.00 On site with the torch in the other hand

You are in the cupboard with the meter in it. The torch is on. The customer is asking what the job will cost. You do not climb down to type with one thumb.

You open a quote from the plus button then tap Talk. The form opens empty on today's date.

You say the job in your own words. The customer and the work and the items and the day and the price can come in the order you think of them.

You say quote for 12 Station Road. Replace stopcock and fit isolation valve. Labour half day £240. Brass fittings £45. Call-out £40. Due Friday.

Each finished sentence is read on its own. You keep talking while the line before it is still being read. The line files itself. An item goes into Materials or Labour or Travel time or Additional charges. The call-out fee keeps its own place. A customer name and an address and the work day and a note go into their own boxes. The app works out the total from the lines as they arrive.

Check the form. A line the reader could not place stays in your own words with one question. Where does this go. You tap a section or delete it. Nothing is guessed and nothing is dropped. A line with no figure stays unpriced on the quote for you to set. A name that only looks like a saved customer is asked about rather than assumed.

The price is the one you say. The app does not keep a rate card and fill in your prices.

When the form looks right you press Make quote. The microphone stops. The reads finish. The quote is saved as a draft. It opens on the quote document. That press never sends it.

The quote carries your business name and address and the customer name and address and the invoice date and a unique quote number and a description with quantity and unit price and line amount and the total. Where you are VAT registered and the VAT amount is above zero the VAT row prints. UK VAT has three rates. 20 percent standard and 5 percent reduced and 0 percent zero rated. Read those rates on gov.uk on 24 October 2026. The threshold is £90,000 in a rolling 12 months since 1 April 2024. Read that on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.

## 10.15 Sent before you leave the drive

You send the quote from the document. The share message and the customer's link both carry the same details. The link sits at link.yellowtradie.com as /q with the quote code. It shows the lines and the figures and the VAT you set and the total. It shows the bank block you set in Settings where you take bank transfer.

The payment note you set in Settings sits under the total. It starts as Payment is due on completion by bank transfer before I leave site. Keep it or change it to your own words.

The customer opens the link. They can approve or decline it there. If they answer by phone you write it down.

There is a second way to speak a quote. Ask the assistant for a quote and it answers with a card before anything is saved. The card is called Make quote. It names the customer and the work and each line with the price it heard. It says when a line could not be read and when a price was not said. Nothing is written until you tap Make quote on the card. Then the draft is saved and the chat gives you one button to open it. If you cancel nothing is saved.

Sending is a separate tap. The customer sees the lines you spoke and the total you set. The Home screen shows the quote as Pending. The small badge shows Draft or Sent. A pending quote that has gone quiet shows Sent 9 days ago or Expires in N days on Home.

## 11.30 The job starts itself

At 11.20 the landlord taps approve on the link. The app records the answer. The quote status moves to Approved. The job is created from the quote with the same title and the same customer and the same address and the same dates.

The job page holds what the work is and where it is and the photos and notes and costs and the bill at the end. You put the job dates in when you quote. A multi day job shows on each day it runs. On its start date the job moves itself to In Progress so you do not have to mark it.

On site the job holds the rest of the day.

You add a note by talking. You add a cost by talking. You add hours on the job by tapping the timesheet and adding start and end. Hours on a job can become a labour line on the invoice. A direct job with no quote still keeps its hours. You take a photo of the meter and of the old valve and of the finished work. The job page is the folder for that work. Photos and notes sit with the money so you can answer a question weeks later without driving back.

Busy days in the diary stop double booking. A job that runs over more than one day shows on each day. A call you missed shows at the top of Today so you can deal with it first.

## 13.00 Lunch and the money question

Between lifts you want a quick answer. You open Ask AI from the dock.

You ask how much was I paid this month. Or who still owes me. Or what jobs are on today. Or what quotes have gone quiet. Jev decides which report you meant. It returns an id for the report and the time window and the customer name where you named one. It never returns a figure.

The phone resolves the window on your own clock and reads your records and builds the answer. A money answer goes through the one money function the app uses for every money screen. The answer is drawn as a card or a table or a chart. A single figure is a card with the label and the amount and a note that says how many payments that covers. A list of bills is a table with invoice and customer and owed and days late. Money over months is a six month chart ending in the month you asked about.

Every number is read from the phone's own database. If there are no records for that period the answer says nothing is there. No number is taken from the model's reply. A figure appears in the money label and in the table cells and bars never in the model's own sentence alone.

It can also do small tasks one at a time with a card that says what it will do. Add a job or add a customer or record a chase and similar short writes. Each is shown as a confirmation card that says what will be saved and where. You tap to allow it. Nothing is written without that tap.

You can open a record from a report. A row can link to the real job or quote or invoice.

## 16.00 The invoice before you drive home

The work is done. The customer is happy. You do not wait until the evening.

From the job you tap Invoice it. A bill is built from the job with the same lines and the same total and the same customer and the same address. Each line carries description and quantity and unit price and amount. VAT rate and VAT amount show per line and in totals where you are VAT registered. The invoice number is the payment reference and it is unique and runs on from the last one.

The invoice carries the bank details you set in Settings. Account name and bank name and sort code and account number each on its own line. Under the account number the reference line reads Reference and the invoice number. On the customer's link the sort code and the account number and the reference each have a Copy control.

The share message carries the link and the amount and the reference. The customer's page for that bill sits at link.yellowtradie.com as /done with the invoice code. It shows the bill and a box to confirm the work was done. The bank details sit on that page with the reference so a customer who wants to transfer can do it from their phone.

Where you are a subcontractor to a CIS contractor the invoice carries the CIS block with labour and materials shown apart at the rate held. That snapshot is written when you raise the invoice and does not change if you later edit the customer. CIS is held from labour only and sent to HMRC as tax. VAT is charged on sales and reclaimed on purchases. The two are worked in separate boxes.

You send the bill straight away.

## 16.10 Paid while you are still on site

The customer says they will pay now. They open the link and copy the sort code and the account number and the reference. They place the transfer with the invoice number as reference.

You tap Record payment on the bill when the money lands. Choose the amount and the date and the method. For a small domestic job where they hand you cash you record it the same way. The bill shows money in and the balance and the word Open or Paid. The Home totals and the reports read the log not the invoice total.

If the customer says they will pay later name the date before you leave. Say the due date out loud. Put it on the invoice. The chase ladder in the app starts from there.

Statutory interest on a late business debt is 8 percent a year above the Bank of England base rate. Read that on gov.uk on 24 October 2026. Fixed sums are £40 on debts up to £999.99 and £70 on debts of £1,000 to £9,999.99 and £100 on debts of £10,000 or more. Read those bands on gov.uk on 24 October 2026. The late payment interest calculator shows the sum. The payment reminder templates give the words. The interest rules depend on whether the customer is a business and what you wrote in your terms before the work started. For a consumer in their own home interest depends on your contract terms and on what is fair under consumer law.

Card on your phone is not in this build today. The bill hides the card row and Settings says the feature is not in this build. When it is live the customer will tap their card on the back of your phone and the app will write a payment only when the provider hands back a signed paid answer for the exact balance. Until then you can take a card in a separate app and tap Record payment on the bill.

## 19.00 Evening without the kitchen table

You are home. The quote that would have waited until the evening was sent at 10.15. The bill that would have waited until the night was sent at 16.00. The photos and the notes are on the job. The payments log is right. The tax estimate is a view from those records in bands with Class 4 National Insurance and CIS already deducted. It carries the line an estimate from your records not advice.

The spoke line is the one that changed the day. You talked and the app wrote. Nothing was typed with one thumb on a ladder and nothing was forgotten by the evening.
