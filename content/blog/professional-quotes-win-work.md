# Professional quotes win work

> **Key facts**
>
> * You must register for VAT when taxable turnover goes over £90,000 in a rolling 12 months since 1 April 2024. Read on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.
> * UK VAT has three rates. 20 percent standard and 5 percent reduced and 0 percent zero rated. Read on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.
> * CIS deductions are 20 percent where the subcontractor is verified and 30 percent where not and 0 percent where gross payment status is held. Read on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.
> * National Living Wage is £12.21 an hour for workers aged 21 and over from April 2025. Read on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.

A quote is a promise the customer can trust. A poor quote leaves the customer guessing. The guessing becomes a no. This post says what makes a quote get accepted and the three things that lose it.

No figure below is invented. Each rate was read on gov.uk on 24 October 2026. The link sits at the foot so you can check it.

## What makes a quote get accepted

### It names the work so the customer can picture it

Write lines the customer can see. First fix and second fix and testing for an electrical job. Strip out and tile and grout for a bathroom. Supply of consumer unit and disposal of waste where those apply.

Put a short sentence under any line that carries an assumption. Note access or making good or parking where those affect time. The customer should know what they pay for without asking.

Keep the contingency inside the total. Do not list it as a line. The customer pays a price that holds reality. They do not need to see your buffer.

### It shows the maths that can be checked

Show subtotal and VAT where you charge it and total. Show how VAT was worked where the rate is 20 percent or 5 percent or 0 percent. Read those rates on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.

Say whether CIS applies. It does not apply to a private householder. It applies on labour only where the customer is a contractor. Materials sit outside the deduction. The deduction is 20 percent where the subcontractor is verified and 30 percent where not verified and 0 percent where gross status is held. Read those rates on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.

Put the payment note under the total. State when payment is due. State how to pay. The best quote answers the next question before the customer asks it.

### It is legally sound

Your business name and address must be correct. Quote number and date and customer name and site address must be present. The total must be clear. The period the price holds must be named. For example 30 days is common.

If you are VAT registered you must show your VAT number on the invoice that follows. You must have registered when taxable turnover went over £90,000 in a rolling 12 months since 1 April 2024. Read that threshold on gov.uk on 24 October 2026.

Labour on site must be priced above cost. National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over is £12.21 an hour from April 2025. Read that rate on gov.uk on 24 October 2026. Your day rate should cover wage and tax and travel and profit. A quote that ignores those costs is funded from your own pocket.

Include how to accept. A link the customer can tap is best. The app gives that link for every quote.

### It looks like a document you would trust with your own home

Use one font at two sizes. Headings in bold. Numbers right aligned. Enough white space. No colour where black will do. The logo and contact details sit at the top. The terms sit at the foot.

Spell the customer name correctly. Match the job title to what the customer asked for. A quote that names Mrs Patel and 12 Station Road for a boiler service is not a template. It is her job.

Send the same day. A quote sent in an hour wins more than a perfect quote sent in a week. Customers choose speed close to certainty.

## The three things that lose it

### One. It leaves the price open

A range is not a price. A price with plus extras to be agreed is not a price. A price that excludes VAT without saying so is not a price. Each forces the customer to guess. Guesswork goes to the next trader.

State the price as one total. State whether VAT is included. State what is included and what is not. If you exclude decorating after chasing into plaster say so. If you exclude skip hire say so. If you exclude materials the customer will supply say so.

If you expect changes price them as variations in writing. Do not carry them as vague extra costs.

### Two. It hides how and when to pay

Customers lose quotes that do not answer payment. How to pay and when to pay must be on the page.

Name the method. Bank transfer with the account name and sort code and account number. State the reference as the invoice number that will follow. The customer puts that reference on the transfer so you can match it.

Name the timing. Fourteen days is common on domestic work. Due on completion is common on small jobs. Staged payments suit larger jobs. Name the deposit where you take one.

Name the period the price holds. Name that acceptance is in writing by link or reply. That stops second guessing on both sides.

### Three. It reads as a list of your costs

Customers choose the value of the finished work. A quote that lists your costs and wages and fuel and margin feels like an account of what you need. That is not what they buy.

Show lines for the work and the total they pay. Keep the cost build behind the scenes. The app lets you build price from days and materials and paperwork and contingency. The customer sees the finished price. You keep the workings.

Example. Three days on site at your day rate plus £600 materials plus £80 paperwork plus 5 percent contingency is the price you hold in your head. The quote shows supply and fit and testing and waste with unit prices that add to that total. The customer sees what they get. The maths is yours.

Another note on contingency. Old buildings and roofs and ground works need more than domestic painting. Hold a higher buffer inside the total where the job is less certain. Do not list the buffer.

## A short check before you send

* Customer name and site address correct and spelled right
* Quote number and date present
* Lines named so the work can be pictured
* Subtotal and VAT where you charge it and total add up
* CIS shown as on labour only where it applies with the right rate from gov.uk
* VAT number shown where you are registered above £90,000
* Price valid for a named period such as 30 days
* Payment note under total with method and reference and due date
* How to accept shown as link or reply
* Sent the same day where you can

Tick these and you have a quote a customer can trust.
