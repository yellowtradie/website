            </span>

              <span class="step-line">The job is in your diary with the address, the notes and the photos. One job moves to In Progress on its own start date.</span>
            </span>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step">
          <div class="step-row">

            </span>

        </div>

          <div class="step-row">
