# Compare YellowTradie with what you use now

If you are choosing a way to run jobs, you want a straight answer. This section gives it. Each comparison starts with what the other option does well, then shows the two side by side, and says when we are the better fit.

We pick comparisons from how solo traders actually work. Most start on a spreadsheet. Others are already paying for a job app. We write the ones that matter to that choice first.

## Every comparison

- [How we compare](/compare/how-we-compare.html): our rules for what we check, how we price check, and that we sell one of the two.
- [YellowTradie against a spreadsheet](/compare/yellowtradie-vs-spreadsheets.html): the honest case for a sheet that is free and does not go down, and what it cannot do for you.
- [YellowTradie against Tradify](/compare/yellowtradie-vs-tradify.html): the biggest rival by users, and where the two differ for a one person business.

More comparisons are on the way. Each one reads the other product on the day it is written and carries the date so you can check it.

## How we pick them

We pick by use. The spreadsheet comes first because most solo traders use one. The job apps come next by how many UK traders use them. We do not write a comparison to fill a gap in a search list. We write it because a trader asked the question.

## What you will find on each page

Each page names the other product's strength first and says it fairly. It gives a short verdict that names a winner. It lists when we are the better fit so you can see if that matches your week. The table shows the facts checked on the same day. The section that follows says where the other product is ahead. That part is there to earn trust. The page ends with four to six questions that traders ask after reading the table.

Every price in a table carries the date it was checked. The rival's price is read off the rival's own pricing page on that day. If a price cannot be checked on that day, the row says so. We do not guess. You can open the same pricing page from Sources and see the figure for yourself.
