## How posts are written

Each post carries a key facts box at the top with three or four sourced numbers. The byline names no person. Sources sit at the foot with a link and a line saying what each source is. Related guides sit under that.

No figure is invented. If the number is not in a source it is not in the post. Every rate was read on gov.uk or legislation.gov.uk or bankofengland.co.uk on the date on the page.
