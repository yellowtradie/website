A day on YellowTradie

The day, in the order it happens

You miss a call on the ladder. By the time you are back on the ground, the day is already set.

1

The call you missed

The caller leaves a message, it is written out and sent to you. If they hung up without a word, they get a text back saying you will ring them. You call back the ones worth your time.
2

You talk, the quote is written

Speak it into the phone in the van. The lines, the rates and the VAT are already there. Check it and send it.
3

You do the work

The job is in your diary with the address, the notes and the photos. One job moves to In Progress on its own start date.
4

The invoice goes out

Ready the moment the job is done. Deposits and part payments are worked out, with the balance and your bank details on it.
5

You get paid

One tap sends the reminder when it is late. The amount still owed is on it, not the original total.
6

The money is in your reports

Every invoice and cost is already logged, so the quarter is ready when you are. Your tax is in bands, with the estimate shown.
