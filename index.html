The problem

The paperwork waits until after tea

You finish the job, drive home, eat, then sit down and write a quote from memory. Half the detail is gone. The price is a guess. The customer waits two days for a number and rings someone else.

You miss a call while you are up a ladder and never ring back. A quote sits unanswered for a week. An invoice goes unpaid because chasing it is the worst hour of your week.

What it does

One phone does the lot

YellowTradie is not a quoting app with a few extras bolted on. It follows the way a one man business actually runs, from the missed call to the money in the bank and the quarter closed off.

Win the work

Catch the missed call

You are up a ladder and the phone rings. YellowTradie answers it for you and texts the caller back, so the job does not walk off to whoever picked up first.

Every enquiry in one list

Missed calls, messages and new leads land in one inbox. Urgent ones are marked, so you know who to ring first when your hands are free.

Know when the quote is read

Send a quote and see when it was opened, approved, changed or turned down. No more wondering whether it landed.

Quote it

Talk the job through

Stand in the room and say what needs doing. YellowTradie writes it down and turns it into line items with prices.

Your rates, your numbers

Your hourly rate, material markup and call-out fee are already in the app. Every quote comes out priced your way, with VAT added if you are registered.

A proper quote, sent in a minute

Send a tidy PDF by text, WhatsApp or email, with your logo, your terms and your payment details on it. Re-price a job and the old version stays on the job.

Plan the week

Your diary on your phone

Book the job in while you are still standing with the customer. The calendar shows busy days, jobs that run over more than one day and what is coming next.

A morning list

Open the app with your brew and see every job on today, with the booked time, the address and the money on it.

Straight to the door

Tap to ring the customer, send them a message or open the address in your maps app. No copying numbers out of a text thread.

Run the job

Photos and notes on the job

Snap the before and after. Dictate a note on site instead of writing it on the back of your hand. It all stays with the job.

Costs as you go

Log the materials, the skip and the fuel against the job while the receipt is still in your pocket. Add the VAT on the receipt when you are registered.

A timesheet for hourly work

For the jobs you never quoted, log your start and finish times. The hours are priced at your rate and pulled into the invoice.

Get paid

Invoice it in a minute

The job is already in the app. Turn it into an invoice and send it as a PDF with your bank details, your terms and the amount owed.

Deposits and part payments

Take a deposit up front and the balance when you finish. Every payment is logged against the invoice, so the balance is always right.

Chase the money

The app puts the late invoices and the quiet quotes at the top and writes the reminder for you. Check it, tap send, done.

Tax and the accountant

The quarterly tax return, covered

Making Tax Digital means reporting your income every quarter. YellowTradie keeps money in, money out, your taxable profit and the tax to set aside, with the days left in the quarter.

Export the lot to your accountant

Send the quarter as a spreadsheet, or export your data for QuickBooks, Xero or FreeAgent. Your accountant gets the same numbers in a fraction of the time.

VAT sorted

Set your VAT rate once and it goes on every quote and invoice. The report shows what you owe HMRC and what you can claim back.

Everything else

Set up in four steps

Your trade, your business name, your rates, your bank details. Five minutes and it is ready to quote.

Questions

Straight answers

What does YellowTradie do?
It runs a one man trade business from a phone. It catches missed calls, turns what you say into a priced quote, books the job into your diary, keeps the photos and costs, invoices the job, chases what is late and works out the figures for your quarterly tax.
Do I need to be good with phones?
If you can send a text and take a photo, you can use this. Every screen does one job. That is the whole test we build against.
Does it work on iPhone?
Yes. YellowTradie is built for Android and iPhone.
How does quoting by voice work?
Press record and talk through the job the way you would explain it to a mate. The app writes it down, sorts it into materials and labour, prices it at your rates and shows you the quote to check before you send it.
What happens when I miss a call on site?
The app answers the call and sends the caller a text so they know you are on the tools. The enquiry lands in your inbox with the number and the time, and you ring back when you are free.
Can I take a deposit and part payments?
Yes. Ask for a deposit up front and the balance when the work is done. Every payment is logged against the invoice, so the balance is always correct.
What message does it send when an invoice is late?
A short, polite reminder with the invoice number, the job, the amount outstanding and your bank details. You can write your own wording in the settings, and nothing is sent until you tap send.
Does it work with QuickBooks, Xero or FreeAgent?
You can export your income, your expenses and your quarter as a spreadsheet for your accountant, or send your data straight to QuickBooks, Xero or FreeAgent. Either way, your accountant gets clean numbers instead of a carrier bag of receipts.
Where does my customer's information live?
On your phone. Your jobs, prices and customers stay in the app on your device.
What does it cost?
Nothing during early access. Pricing is not decided yet, but early users get a low rate locked in when it goes paid, because you helped shape it. We will tell you before anything costs money.
When do I get in?
We are letting people in a few at a time so we can answer every message and use what you tell us. Leave your email and we will come to you.
Is it built for teams or for one person?
One person. One phone. There is no user list to manage and no office login. If you need to allocate jobs across a team, this is not for you yet.
