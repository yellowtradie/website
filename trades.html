Built for your trade, not for an office

One app for one person on site, phone in hand. It does not matter if you are pulling cable or laying block. The day has the same shape: a call you could not take, a price you have to name before you start, a bill you have to send before you drive home. YellowTradie keeps that day in one place.

Pick the trade that is yours below. Each page shows the same app through that trade's day.

The six trades

  • For electricians: the call you missed, the job you have to price before you can start, and the bill you send before you drive home.
  • For plumbers: the emergency call, the job you price on arrival, and the deposit you need before you order the part.
  • For builders: the quote for the whole job, the change the customer asks for half way through, and the stage payment that keeps you paid.
  • For roofers: the survey from the loft, the scaffold and the weather window, and the photograph of the valley.
  • For decorators: the quote by room, the prep time people forget, and the finish the customer cannot see until the light changes.
  • For landscapers: the quote by area, the materials by volume, and the weather window you have to work inside.

Every one of the six is the same app. The features do not change. The day does.

What the app does, in short

You speak the quote and it fills itself line by line. You send it from your phone and the customer answers on a link with no account and no password. When they say yes the job starts. Photos and costs and hours sit with that job. The invoice comes from the job and carries your bank details and the invoice number as the payment reference. You record what was paid and the balance falls. The chase asks for what is still owed. Your records stay on your phone.

Read the full list on every part of the app.

Who it is not for

It is not for a team. There is no second login and no way to give the job to someone else. It is for one person running the whole job from one phone.
